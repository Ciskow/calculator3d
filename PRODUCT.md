# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person: the owner of a small 3D printing operation in Brazil, pricing their own prints. They sit at a desktop monitor next to the slicer, copy time and weight from it, and want a sale price. Phone use is occasional.

## Product Purpose

Turns print time, filament weight and accessories into a true cost per unit, then into consumer and reseller (lojista) prices with the net profit after tax, card fee and ad cost. Success is pricing a piece in under a minute and trusting the number.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, exact. A workshop instrument, closer to a slicer's settings panel or Linear than to a marketing dashboard. Numbers lead; decoration is nearly absent. The interface language is Brazilian Portuguese.

## Anti-references

- The previous version: stacked rounded cards in a centred 1200px column, uppercase tracked labels on every section, emoji as icons.
- SaaS dashboards built from big-number KPI cards.
- Roomy, friendly small-business apps with illustration and guidance copy.

## Design Principles

1. Everything needed to price a piece is visible at once; no scrolling between inputs and the resulting price.
2. Numbers are the content: tabular figures, right-aligned, consistent currency formatting.
3. Every figure can be traced: each cost line shows what it was computed from.
4. The maths never changes silently; results match the owner's spreadsheet.
5. Density over decoration, with one accent for action and one colour for cost.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme. Full keyboard operation of every input, visible focus rings, reduced-motion respected. Profit and loss are never signalled by colour alone (a minus sign always accompanies red).
